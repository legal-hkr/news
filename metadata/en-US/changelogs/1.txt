v0.1.0

First version:
- RSS and Atom feeds, in folders, with OPML import and export
- Full text and images stored on the phone, for reading without a network
- One list with filters: source, unread only, saved only, and search
- Reader with adjustable text size and typeface, and an image viewer
- Refresh in the background, without notifications
- Backup and restore of feeds, settings and saved articles
